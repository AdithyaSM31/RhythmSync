Window behaviour

  • Custom title bar: dark, with the app icon and name, plus minimize, maximize and close. Close hides to the tray, as today. Windows 11 snap layouts and the rounded corners still work.
  • Flyout position: bottom-right of the working area on the monitor with the tray, so it sits above the taskbar wherever the taskbar is. It respects DPI scaling. Only one flyout at a time; clicking the icon again closes it.
  • Flyout volume sliders drive the same host per-device volume as the main window (one source of truth, saved the same way).
  • Keyboard: everything reachable with Tab, a visible violet focus ring, and Esc closes the flyout.
